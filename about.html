Who we are

Mount Valley Public School

🎯 Mission

Our mission is to create a dynamic, inclusive, and engaging learning environment that inspires innovation, cultivates leadership, and equips students to thrive in a rapidly changing world by fostering creativity, adaptability, collaboration, resilience, and critical thinking. We are committed to nurturing curiosity, embracing diversity, integrating modern technology, and promoting lifelong values that prepare students to grow both academically and personally.

🌟 Vision

Our vision is to prepare students for both academic excellence and real-life challenges, empowering them to become lifelong learners, problem-solvers, innovators, and responsible global citizens. We aim to shape future leaders who embody integrity, empathy, and courage, while contributing positively to society. Through transformative education, cutting-edge practices, and holistic development, we strive to create individuals who can adapt, lead, and inspire change in a rapidly evolving world.

Empowering Every Student

Mount Valley Public School

Mount Valley Public School was established in 2021 under the guidance of the JM EDUCATIONAL TRUST, with the vision of providing high-quality education that blends knowledge with values. What began as a modest institution with a few classrooms has now grown into a full-fledged ICSE school. Over the years, the school has consistently focused on academic excellence, holistic development, and character building. With a team of dedicated teachers, modern infrastructure, science and computer laboratories, a well-stocked library, and a spacious playground, the school has become a center of learning and growth.Today, Mount Valley Public School proudly serves [200 students ] students, offering a balanced curriculum that emphasizes academics, sports, arts, Robotics,spoken English and co-curricular activities. Rooted in strong values and inspired by our vision, the school continues to prepare students to face the challenges of tomorrow with confidence and integrity.

ICSE Curriculum Overview

The Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) is conducted by the CISCE, New Delhi. It offers a balanced, application-based, and globally recognized education system. It is designed to give students a balanced education with strong emphasis on ENGLISH, MATHEMATICS, SCIENCE, SOCIAL STUDIES & CO-CURRICULAR SUBJECTS.

Language-rich

Strong focus on English + a second language (Hindi/regional/foreign).

Balanced Subjects

Equal weightage to Science, Math, Humanities, and Arts.

Internal Assessments

20–30% marks from projects and practicals.

Global Recognition

Accepted by top universities in India & abroad.

Subjects Offered

Classes I – VIII (School-based curriculum aligned to ICSE)
  • English (Language + Literature)
  • Second Language (Hindi/regional/foreign)
  • Mathematics
  • Science (General Science up to Class VI; Physics, Chemistry, Biology from Class VII)
  • Social Studies (History, Civics, Geography)
  • Computer Applications / Environmental Studies
  • Art, Craft, Moral Science, Physical Education
Classes IX – X (ICSE Board Exam)

Students must study 6–7 subjects in 3 groups:

Group I (Compulsory – Internal & External Exams):

  • English (Language + Literature)
  • Second Language (Hindi/other approved)
  • History, Civics & Geography

Group II (Choose any 2):

  • Mathematics
  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Economics, Commercial Studies, Environmental Science
  • Modern Foreign Language, Classical Language

Group III (Choose 1):

  • Computer Applications, Economic Applications
  • Commercial Applications, Performing Arts (Music, Dance, Drama)
  • Physical Education, Environmental Applications
  • Home Science, Technical Drawing, Fashion Designing, Yoga, Cookery
🏆 Assessment System

Class IX → School exams (CISCE pattern).

Class X → ICSE Board Exam (CISCE).

Marks Split:

  • 80% Written Exam
  • 20% Internal Assessment / Project Work
